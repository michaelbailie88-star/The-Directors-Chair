WHAT THIS IS

Confessional-cast filmmaking.

Every film here is built entirely out of solo, self-recorded confessionals — cast members talking straight to their own camera, never appearing in the same shot, never in the same room as anyone else in the story. No set. No crew. No budget required from you.

You bring the idea. If it's chosen, I write out exactly what each character needs to say, cast real people to play them, and cut their footage together into a finished short — published under your name as the writer.

HOW IT WORKS

Four scenes, start to finish.

01Submit

You send the premise.

A short story idea, the characters in it, and what kind of film it should feel like — comedy, drama, thriller, whatever fits. That's the whole ask.

02Casting

If it's chosen, casting opens.

A casting call goes out for the characters in your story. Real people apply to play them — strangers who'll each shoot their part alone, on their own phone.

03The Packet

Each cast member gets their own direction.

Not a full script — a private packet built just for their character: who they are, what to say, and sample lines to work from. Nobody sees anyone else's.

04Wrap

It gets cut together and released on YouTube.

Every confessional comes back to me, gets edited into one film, and goes out publicly on my YouTube channel. Both the writer and every cast member are credited in the video's description.
